Methodology
Gas

Actual gallons and dollars from each fill receipt are assigned to that tank.

Home charging

Home charging is valued at a standardized 16.0608¢/kWh summer rate and a provisional 14.0391¢/kWh winter rate. Tank #6 splits charging across the October 1 seasonal change; solar and net-metering credits are excluded.

Public charging

Paid public charging uses the actual session cost.

¢/mi
Energy cost per mile

Total energy cost (gasoline, home charging, and paid public charging) is divided by tracked miles.

2018 benchmark

The same miles are modeled at 22 MPG using the Twin Cities average gas-price benchmark for each tank period.

Home charging electricity rates

Home charging energy costs are estimated using the Xcel Energy residential rate structure in effect when charging occurred. Before October 18, 2026, the project uses seasonal rates of 16.0608¢/kWh in summer and a provisional 14.0391¢/kWh in winter. Tank #6 charging is split between September 30 (12.112 kWh at the summer rate) and October 1–15 (155.200 kWh at the winter rate). On October 18, 2026, the household switched to Time-of-Use (TOU) billing; subsequent home charging is valued using the applicable TOU period rate. Earlier tank costs are not recalculated for the switch. These are attributed charging energy costs, not necessarily the household's net electricity bill after solar generation and net-metering credits. The provisional winter rate will be checked against the first bill reflecting October usage.

What this project is measuring

The focus is operating energy cost: actual gasoline plus actual paid public charging plus home electricity valued at the applicable residential rate, compared with estimated operating costs for a 2018 Chrysler Pacifica. Home charging costs reflect the applicable Xcel Energy residential electricity charges during each tank period, including standard or Time-of-Use rates as applicable. Solar production and net-metering credits are excluded.
